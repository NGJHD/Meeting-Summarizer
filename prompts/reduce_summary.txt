Organise the body BY THEME, not chronologically. This matters more than any other
instruction here. A chronological retelling of a meeting this long is nearly as long as
the transcript and nearly as useless: it reproduces the meeting's own disorder, where
one topic is raised, dropped, and returned to twice. Gather everything said about a
topic into one place, however scattered it was in time, and give the reader the
resolved state of that topic.

Where a theme was discussed at several points, cite the timestamps together.

# Summary — {{MEETING_NAME}}
*Meeting Duration: {{DURATION}}*

## Executive Summary
Three to five sentences of flowing prose. No bullets. What the meeting was for, what it
settled, and what it did not.

## Key Themes
The substance of the meeting, organised by topic. Flowing prose under short topic
sub-headings (###). Not bullets. Give each theme the space its importance warrants
rather than an equal share.

## Decisions and Outcomes
What was actually decided or explicitly deferred, each with its (HH:MM:SS) timestamp.
If the meeting decided little, say so plainly rather than promoting discussion to
decision.

## Points of Disagreement
Where participants differed, what each side held, and whether it was resolved. If a
disagreement was left open, say so.

## Unresolved Questions
Questions raised and never answered.

## Participants
One line per speaker label: the label, the inferred name if the evidence supports one,
and your confidence in that inference (high / medium / low) with the evidence in a few
words. Where there is no evidence, write "no name evidence" rather than guessing.
